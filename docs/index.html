SHIFTSCAPE

Outrun the Void. Flip the world. Rewind your mistakes.
A 3D escape game built in Unity 6 — browser, Android and PC.

What makes it different

The Void

A wall of nothing creeps behind you through every world. It pauses in puzzle rooms so you can think — but outside them, keep moving.

Gravity flips

Flip pads send you running on the ceiling, past floor hazards, and back again. The camera flips with you.

Rewind

Hold R to scrub back the last 3 seconds of your own movement. Charges refill at every checkpoint.

Always new

Every run is generated from a seed: platform hops, spinner gauntlets, crumbling bridges, bounce towers, memory pads, code vaults and crate puzzles.

Daily Shift

One seed per day for everyone. Same rooms, same shards — compare your time with friends.

Ghost race

Your best run on each world is saved as a translucent ghost. Race it on the same seed and beat it.

Workshop & Wardrobe

Spend Shards on Pulse Boots (double jump), the Drift Cape (glide), Chrono Coil (+rewind) and more. Suits, hats and trails are cosmetic only.

Five worlds

Sky Foundry, Frost Observatory (ice!), Neon Arcade, Sunken Temple and Clockwork Spire — unlocked with stars.
